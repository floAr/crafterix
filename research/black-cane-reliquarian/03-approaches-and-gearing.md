# 03 — Approaches & gearing detail

Socket budget (standard bases): **Soulwrest 6L + body 6L + Dark Monarch helm 4L + gloves
4L + boots 4L = 24 sockets** (two 6-links). Rings/amulet/belt = no sockets.

## Mandatory gems on this chassis (any main skill)

- **Desecrate** generator (+ Spell Cascade / Unleash) — feeds the corpse loop.
- A **corpse consumer** (this *is* Soulwrest's phantasm trigger). If the main skill
  consumes corpses (VD), it doubles as the consumer.
- **Socketed Summon Phantasm Support (+ Congregation Support)** — the phantasm cap/scaling
  engine (dilutes whatever link it sits in).
- **Driver:** Cyclone + CwC (channel), or Arcanist Brand.
- Movement (Flame Dash + Second Wind), guard (CWDT + Vaal Molten Shell), curse/exposure
  (via Arcanist Brand — **totems may be blocked by Dark Monarch**, see `04`).
- Auras: **Discipline** mandatory on mid-ES; + Grace/Determination + Enlighten.

## Plan A — cold Volatile Dead (RECOMMENDED)

**Core link (Soulwrest 6L):**
`Cyclone – Cast while Channelling – Volatile Dead – Desecrate – Cold Penetration – Greater Spell Cascade`
- Keep **Desecrate and VD at level 1** (cheap frequent casts; low corpse-life so orbs
  don't out-range). One CwC tick: Desecrate generates → VD consumes (damage hit carrying
  the packet) → same consumption triggers Soulwrest phantasms. **Damage + phantasm
  sustain = one action, one held key.**

**Conversion & scaling:**
- **100% phys→cold** via Hrimsorrow (50%) + Watcher's Eye Hatred "% phys as extra/converted
  cold" and/or rare "% of phys converted to cold" gloves.
- Amps: **Hypothermia** (more vs chilled), **Frostbite** + **Cold Exposure** (Wave of
  Conviction set to cold, auto via Arcanist Brand) + **Cold Penetration** → ~−80 to −120
  effective cold res. Crit optional (a "Hybrid Crit VD Reliquarian" variant exists).

**Defense (mid-ES):**
- **Discipline + Watcher's Eye "gain ES per enemy hit"** (Cyclone hits constantly →
  near-continuous recovery — top recovery-per-cost) + **Vaal Discipline** (recharge can't
  be delayed). **Glorious Vanity → Corrupted Soul** funds the ES pool. Mageblood when
  affordable. Freeze/shatter as clear defense.
- **Avoid** Petrified Blood / Progenesis-for-life (dead on ES). Treat **Eldritch Battery**
  as offense (only on CI/LL), not defense.

**Ergonomics:** ~2–3 keys (hold Cyclone; Flame Dash; Arcanist Brand upkeep). Fits with a
6-link to spare.

## Plan B — physical BV + Blade Blast of Unloading + Impale (enthusiast)

**Only build that keeps the packet physical for Impale.** Higher ceiling, strained here.

- **Use REGULAR Blade Vortex (10 blades)**, not of-the-Scythe.
- **Entropic Devastation gloves** gate it → **crit build required** + glove-lock (costs
  conversion%, ES, resists). Impale capped without Champion.

**Tightest realistic layout (no Saboteur, no totems):**
- **Soulwrest 6L (damage):** Blade Blast of Unloading – Summon Phantasm Support –
  Congregation Support – Controlled-Destruction-or-Increased-Crit – Phys-to-Lightning/Elem –
  (last support). *2 of 6 links spent on the phantasm engine → only ~3 true damage supports.*
- **Body 6L (whole engine on one brand):** Arcanist Brand – Blade Vortex – Desecrate –
  Bone/Spirit Offering (consumer) – Wither/Vulnerability – Spell Cascade.
- **Helm 4L:** Brand Recall + Second Wind + CWDT + Vaal Molten Shell.
- **Gloves = Entropic Devastation** (no free sockets for a big aura link).
- **Boots 4L:** Flame Dash + Second Wind + Discipline + Enlighten.

**Costs vs Plan A:**
- Both 6-links consumed (one diluted); aura package thinned on a fragile mid-ES base.
- ~**4 keys, 2 continuous spam** (Blade Blast + Brand Recall), whole engine on one
  Arcanist Brand (dies if brand drops).
- **~⅓–½ detonation throughput** without Triggerbots; bursty manual restack.
- **Automation vs payoff fight:** auto-firing BB (CwC) never lets the stack bank → small
  detonations; the big-stack "Unloading" payoff needs the **manual bank-and-dump** play.

**When Plan B is worth it:** you specifically want the crit-phys single-target playstyle
and accept the key/socket/aura costs. Damage-through-armour is fine (armour ~1–3%); the
downside is purely ergonomics + socket starvation. **Verify the Dark Monarch totem-block
(`04`)** — if fixed in 3.29, Plan B eases (cheap totem automation returns).

## Skill ranking context (why VD/BB and not the "nukes")

For a huge flat-added-per-hit packet, throughput = `effectiveness × hits/sec`. Multi-hit
skills win sustained single-target; high-coefficient "nukes" (Frost Bomb of Forthcoming
460%, Flame Surge of Combusting 410%, Freezing Pulse 370%) are delay/one-hit-limited:
- **Frost Bomb of Forthcoming** — highest nuke ceiling but detonation-delay + no cooldown
  to abuse → burst tool, not a DPS engine.
- **Flame Surge of Combusting** — best *sustained* one-button alternative if not going VD
  (instant spam, "more vs burning"), but it's fire so the physical packet is off-colour
  without phys→fire conversion.
- **Freezing Pulse** — 5 projectiles give ~0 extra single-target (no shotgun); **Nimis/
  return does NOT work** (it pierces + fades). Return caps at +1 same-target hit anyway.
- **Void Sphere of Rending** — comfy mapper; fixed ~3.3 hits/s pulse clock caps
  single-target; conversion double-up (60% skill + Ming's on original base) confirmed.
⚠️ The transfigured coefficients above are content-farm-sourced — verify in PoB (`04`).
